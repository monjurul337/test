## What is the best anti-detect browser in 2026?

There is no universal winner. Compare **Multilogin**, **GoLogin**, **AdsPower**, **Kameleo**, and **Incogniton** on your proxy stack and target platform. Start: [best antidetect browser comparison](../guides/best-antidetect-browser-comparison.md).

## What is an antidetect browser?

Software that isolates **browser profiles** and **fingerprints** so sessions do not leak cookies or device signals between accounts.
