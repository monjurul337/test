# Getting Started with Multilogin (Antidetect Browser)

**Multilogin** helps teams run isolated **browser profiles**—each with its own cookies, storage, and **browser fingerprint**—so workflows stay separated across ads, marketplaces, and social platforms.

## What you need

- A Multilogin account and desktop app (or supported environment per current docs)
- Proxies matched to target geo (residential or mobile where required)
- A clear naming scheme for profiles (client / campaign / region)

## Recommended first steps

1. Create a workspace and your first profile with a realistic OS + timezone + language set.
2. Assign a dedicated proxy; avoid mixing countries on one profile.
3. Launch the profile, log in once, and export backup rules if your team uses them.
4. Scale gradually—validate detection risk on a small batch before hundreds of profiles.

## vs “cheap” antidetect tools

Tools like **GoLogin** or **AdsPower** can work for small tests; teams that outgrow fingerprint leaks or team permissions often evaluate **Multilogin** for mature **profile management** and automation hooks.
