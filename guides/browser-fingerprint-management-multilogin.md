# Browser Fingerprint Management with Multilogin

A **browser fingerprint** combines canvas, WebGL, fonts, timezone, language, screen size, and hardware signals. **Antidetect browsers** like **Multilogin** let each profile present a coherent device story.

## Best practices

- Lock timezone and geolocation to proxy country  
- Avoid randomizing too many parameters per session  
- Keep user-agent and OS family aligned  
- Separate profiles per brand / client—never reuse logins across unrelated fingerprints  

## Why fingerprints matter

Ad platforms and marketplaces correlate sessions. Inconsistent fingerprints—or reuse across accounts—increase review rates. Teams comparing **Multilogin vs GoLogin** or **AdsPower** should test *their* target properties.
