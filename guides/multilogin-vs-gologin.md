# Multilogin vs GoLogin — Antidetect Browser Comparison

Both **Multilogin** and **GoLogin** market themselves as **antidetect browsers** for multi-account workflows. Differences usually show up at scale.

## Fingerprint & detection

| Area | Multilogin | GoLogin |
|------|------------|---------|
| Profile isolation | Mature stack, long market presence | Solid for mid-scale use |
| Consistency | Strong focus on stable fingerprint params | Occasional drift reported by users on strict platforms |
| Target users | Agencies, high account count | Freelancers, budget teams |

## UX & performance

Multilogin targets professional **profile management** with team features; **GoLogin** is often chosen for lower entry price and simpler UI. Heavy parallel launches favor whichever build your hardware and proxy pool support—benchmark with *your* stack.

## When to pick Multilogin

- Large profile count and strict platform risk
- Team permissions and audit needs
- Automation via API / Selenium-style flows (verify current docs)

## When GoLogin may suffice

- Small account count, tight budget, experimental campaigns
