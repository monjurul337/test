Team

Share profile settings with your team using Export / Import — cookies and logins never leave a device.

How sharing works in Moxixo (local edition)

1. Click Export in Settings — you get one moxixo-profiles.json file.
2. Send the file to your teammate (WhatsApp, Drive, email — your choice).
3. They click Import — every profile appears with a fresh, clean session on their device, ready to log in.

✔ Proxy country = timezone = language (keep them matched)

✔ Warm up new accounts slowly before heavy activity
